# BMW N52 Engine - Torque Specifications

## Engine Code: N52B30 (2004-2015)
Used in: E90/E91/E92/E93 325i/328i/330i, E60/E61 525i/528i/530i, E83 X3, E89 Z4

## Critical Torque Values

### Cylinder Head
- **Cylinder Head Bolts** (aluminum block):
  - Stage 1: 22 lb-ft
  - Stage 2: +90° (quarter turn)
  - Stage 3: +90° (quarter turn)
  - **CRITICAL**: Use NEW bolts only (stretch bolts)
  - Part #: 11 12 7 567 219 (set of 14)

### Valve Cover
- **Valve Cover Bolts**: 7 lb-ft (hand tight + snug)
  - **WARNING**: Over-torquing cracks plastic cover
  - Apply thin coat of sealant to gasket groove
  - Torque in cross pattern

### Oil System
- **Oil Pan Drain Plug**: 18 lb-ft
  - Replace crush washer every change
  - Part #: 11 13 7 567 830
- **Oil Filter Housing Cap**: 18 lb-ft
  - Replace O-rings: 11 42 7 525 334 (set)
- **VANOS Solenoid Bolts**: 7 lb-ft
- **Oil Pump Chain Tensioner**: 18 lb-ft + Loctite 243

### Intake/Exhaust
- **Intake Manifold Bolts**: 7 lb-ft (plastic manifold)
- **Exhaust Manifold Nuts**: 15 lb-ft
- **O2 Sensor**: 37 lb-ft (use anti-seize on threads)
- **Spark Plugs**: 18-22 lb-ft (NGK spec: 18 lb-ft)
  - Gap: 0.039-0.043" (1.0-1.1mm)

### Ignition System
- **Ignition Coil Bolts**: 7 lb-ft
  - Part #: 12 13 7 594 938 (Bosch OEM)

### Timing/Front Cover
- **Front Cover Bolts**: 7 lb-ft (M6), 15 lb-ft (M8)
- **Crankshaft Pulley Bolt**:
  - 74 lb-ft + 150° (use NEW bolt)
  - Part #: 11 21 7 548 294

### Accessory Drive
- **Alternator Bracket Bolts**: 31 lb-ft
- **AC Compressor Bolts**: 18 lb-ft
- **Water Pump Bolts**: 7 lb-ft (plastic impeller housing)

### Cooling System
- **Thermostat Housing Bolts**: 7 lb-ft
- **Coolant Hose Clamps**: Hand tight (worm gear style)

## Common Mistakes
1. **Over-torquing plastic components** (valve cover, intake manifold, thermostat)
   - Result: Cracks, leaks
2. **Reusing stretch bolts** (head bolts, crank bolt)
   - Result: Bolt failure, catastrophic damage
3. **Not using thread locker** on oil pump chain tensioner
   - Result: Tensioner backs out, chain jump, engine damage

## Notes for Workshop Use
- Always replace stretch bolts (marked with collar or waist)
- Use BMW-spec coolant only (no green universal)
- Valvetronic motor bolts: 7 lb-ft (fragile plastic housing)
- VANOS solenoids: Replace O-rings when removing
