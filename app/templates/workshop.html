Workshop details

Welcome. The first time: fill in the workshop's own details, add the blank job-sheet picture, give each staff member a login, choose whether you want example jobs to look around with, then start your first job. Everything here can be changed later from “Workshop details”.

  1. The workshop. These are printed on every invoice, exactly as typed here.
  2. Blank job sheet. A scan or clear photo of the empty paper job sheet (PNG or JPEG). Job sheets and invoices are printed on top of it, and the car drawing on screen is cut from it.
  3. Staff logins. Give each person their own login. Their name is then kept on every job they confirm, every invoice they issue and every payment or expense they enter. Staff logins can do the daily work but cannot change this page. A login is switched off, never deleted.
  4. Example jobs (optional). Adds three months of made-up jobs, invoices, payments and expenses in your workshop's name, so every page has something to show. The people and vehicle numbers are invented. Remove them before you start real work: removing takes out every example job with its invoices, payments and expenses, and invoice numbers start again after your last real one.
  5. Backup. Download a copy of everything (jobs, invoices, payments, expenses, photos) at least once a week and keep it off this computer, for example in your e-mail or a USB stick. The free database keeps no copies of its own.

    How to use
    
  
    The steps along the top follow the paper job sheet, left to right. You can click any step at any time.
    
  
      
    
    1. Car & customer. Click New job. Type the job no., vehicle no., make and model, owner, contact and dates. Click the fuel level. If you have a photo of the paper sheet you can attach it; it is optional.
      2. 
    
    2. Knock. Click + Add line for each knocking job and type what is written and the amount. Then click the car picture on the right where the damage is. A number appears; type what it is, for example “rear bumper dent”.
      3. 
    
    3. Repair & parts. Same thing for servicing, repairs and parts, for example “radiator fan motor” or “front oil seal”.
      4. 
    
    4. Spray. Same thing for spray painting, one line per panel or as written on the sheet.
      5. 
    
    5. Check & save. Read the summary. Choose whether it is a quotation, an agreed job or a finished job. Click Save. When you have checked it against the paper or the car, tick the box and click Save and confirm as checked.
      6. 
    
    6. Print & invoice. Click Print job sheet to get the workshop form filled in. For a finished job click Make invoice, work out the totals, issue it, then Print invoice on the workshop form.
      7. 
  
    
  
    Good to know
    
  
      
    
    • No amount yet? Leave it empty. Empty means “not known”, never zero.
      • 
    
    • Cannot read it or not sure what it means? Type what you see and tick Not sure. You can save, but not confirm, until someone clears it up.
      • 
    
    • Line crossed out on the paper? Add it anyway and tick Crossed out. It is kept but not charged.
      • 
    
    • Made a mistake after confirming? Open the job, fix it, save and confirm again. The old version stays in the history.
      • 
    
    • Looking for an old job? Click Find job and search by owner, vehicle no., job no. or any word.
      • 
    
    • Car been here before? Type the vehicle no. on step 1. The owner and contact fill in by themselves and the earlier jobs are listed.
      • 
    
    • Quick lines. On Knock, Repair and Spray, click a grey button such as “Rear bumper” to add that line, then type the amount.
      • 
    
    • Customer paid? On step 6, or in Books, record the amount and how it was paid. A refund is a minus amount.
      • 
    
    • End of the month. Click Books, pick the month and Download for Excel. It lists every invoice with GST, paid and balance.
      • 
    
    • Not sure what a part is called? Click Paper guide for the paper job sheet and the car, lorry and motorbike part names.
      • 
    
    • Invoices. One per job, only for finished jobs, and it cannot be changed once issued. They are numbered in order, for example INV-2026-0001.
      • 
    
    • Job no. already used? The app will not save two jobs with the same job no. Open the earlier one with Find job, or use a new number.
      • 
    
    • Accident job? On step 1 open Accident or insurance job and fill in the insurer, the report no. and what the customer wants to do.
      • 
    
    • Paid a supplier? In Books, under Money paid out, record who was paid, what for and how much. The profit and loss at the bottom updates by itself.
      • 
    
    • Who typed it? Everyone signs in with their own login, shown at the top right. To switch person, close the browser and open it again. The owner adds logins in Workshop details.
      • 
    
    • Workshop name or address wrong on the invoice? Change it in Workshop details. Invoices already issued keep the old details.
