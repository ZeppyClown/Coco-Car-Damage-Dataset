    How to use
    
  
    The steps along the top follow the paper job sheet, left to right. You can click any step at any time.
    
  
      
    
    1. Car & customer. Click New job. Type the job no., vehicle no., make and model, owner, contact and dates. Click the fuel level. If you have a photo of the paper sheet you can attach it; it is optional.
      2. 
    
    2. Knock. Click + Add line for each knocking job and type what is written and the amount. Then click the car picture on the right where the damage is. A number appears; type what it is, for example “rear bumper dent”.
      3. 
    
    3. Repair & parts. Same thing for servicing, repairs and parts, for example “radiator fan motor” or “front oil seal”.
      4. 
    
    4. Spray. Same thing for spray painting, one line per panel or as written on the sheet.
      5. 
    
    5. Check & save. Read the summary. Choose whether it is a quotation, an agreed job or a finished job. Click Save. When you have checked it against the paper or the car, tick the box and click Save and confirm as checked.
      6. 
    
    6. Print & invoice. Click Print job sheet to get the workshop form filled in. For a finished job click Make invoice, work out the totals, issue it, then Print invoice on the workshop form.
      7. 
  
    
  
    Good to know
    
  
      
    
    • No amount yet? Leave it empty. Empty means “not known”, never zero.
      • 
    
    • Cannot read it or not sure what it means? Type what you see and tick Not sure. You can save, but not confirm, until someone clears it up.
      • 
    
    • Line crossed out on the paper? Add it anyway and tick Crossed out. It is kept but not charged.
      • 
    
    • Made a mistake after confirming? Open the job, fix it, save and confirm again. The old version stays in the history.
      • 
    
    • Looking for an old job? Click Find job and search by owner, vehicle no., job no. or any word.
      • 
    
    • Car been here before? Type the vehicle no. on step 1. The owner and contact fill in by themselves and the earlier jobs are listed.
      • 
    
    • Quick lines. On Knock, Repair and Spray, click a grey button such as “Rear bumper” to add that line, then type the amount.
      • 
    
    • Customer paid? On step 6, or in Books, record the amount and how it was paid. A refund is a minus amount.
      • 
    
    • End of the month. Click Books, pick the month and Download for Excel. It lists every invoice with GST, paid and balance.
      • 
    
    • Not sure what a part is called? Click Paper guide for the paper job sheet and the car, lorry and motorbike part names.
      • 
    
    • Invoices. One per job, only for finished jobs, and it cannot be changed once issued.
