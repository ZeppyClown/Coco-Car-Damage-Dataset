# Common Repair Labor Time Guide

## How to Use This Guide

**Labor rates vary by:**
- Geographic region ($80-200/hr typical range)
- Shop type (independent vs dealer vs chain)
- Vehicle complexity (economy vs luxury vs exotic)

**This guide provides:**
- Book time (industry standard hours)
- Real-world time (actual workshop experience)
- Difficulty rating

**Pricing formula:**
```
Labor Cost = Hours × Shop Rate
Total Estimate = Parts + Labor + Shop Supplies (typically 10-15%)
```

---

## Engine - Common Repairs

### **Oil Change**
- **Book Time:** 0.3 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.2-0.4 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Notes:** Add time for skid plate removal, cartridge filters, or hard-to-access drain plugs

**Typical shop pricing:**
- Quick lube: $30-50 (synthetic)
- Independent: $50-80
- Dealer: $80-120

---

### **Spark Plug Replacement**

**4-Cylinder:**
- **Book Time:** 0.5-1.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.5-1.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate
- **Variables:** Coil-on-plug vs plug wires, access

**6-Cylinder (Inline):**
- **Book Time:** 1.0-1.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 1.0-2.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Variables:** Valve cover removal, tight access

**V6:**
- **Book Time:** 1.5-2.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.0-3.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Hard
- **Notes:** Rear bank often requires intake manifold removal

**V8:**
- **Book Time:** 2.0-4.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.5-5.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Notes:** Rear plugs on transverse V8s = nightmare

**Examples:**
- Honda 4-cyl: 0.5 hrs (easy access)
- BMW N52 I6: 1.5 hrs (valve cover off)
- Ford F-150 V8: 2.0 hrs (reasonable access)
- GM 3.6L V6 (transverse): 3.5 hrs (rear bank = manifold removal)

---

### **Timing Belt Replacement**

**4-Cylinder:**
- **Book Time:** 4.0-6.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 4.5-7.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Add:** Water pump (+1 hr), tensioner/pulleys (included)

**V6:**
- **Book Time:** 6.0-8.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 7.0-10.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Add:** Water pump (+1-2 hrs)

**Common examples:**
- Honda 4-cyl (K-series): 4.5 hrs
- VW/Audi 2.0T: 5.5 hrs
- Subaru 4-cyl: 6.5 hrs (engine removal easier)
- Toyota V6 (transverse): 8.5 hrs
- Audi V6 (longitudinal): 9.0 hrs

**Kit pricing:**
- Economy kit: $150-250
- Quality kit with water pump: $300-500
- OEM kit: $400-800

**Total typical cost:** $800-1800 (parts + labor)

---

### **Head Gasket Replacement**

**4-Cylinder:**
- **Book Time:** 8.0-12.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 10.0-16.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Add:** Head resurfacing (required), new head bolts

**V6/V8 (per side):**
- **Book Time:** 10.0-15.0 hrs per side
- **Real Time:** 12.0-20.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Expert Level

**Common examples:**
- Subaru 4-cyl: 12 hrs (engine removal method)
- BMW N52 I6: 14 hrs
- GM 3800 V6: 10 hrs (one side), 18 hrs (both)

**Parts needed:**
- Head gasket set: $150-400
- Head bolts: $50-150
- Coolant, oil: $50
- Machine work (resurface head): $100-200

**Total typical cost:** $1500-3500

## Brakes

### **Brake Pads (Front or Rear)**
- **Book Time:** 0.8-1.2 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.5-1.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate
- **Add:** Rotor replacement (no extra labor if doing pads)

**Pricing:**
- Economy pads: $30-60 per axle
- Quality pads: $60-120 per axle
- Premium/OEM: $100-200 per axle
- Rotors: $40-150 each (average $60)

**Labor cost:** $80-180 per axle

**Total per axle:**
- Economy: $150-300
- Quality: $250-450
- Premium: $350-600

---

### **Brake Rotors + Pads (Both)**
- **Book Time:** 0.8-1.2 hrs (same as pads alone)
- **Real Time:** 1.0-2.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy

**Note:** Rotor replacement adds minimal time when doing pads

---

### **Brake Fluid Flush**
- **Book Time:** 0.5-1.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.5-1.2 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Method:** Pressure bleeder or two-person manual bleed

**Pricing:**
- Brake fluid: $15-30
- Labor: $60-150

---

## Suspension

### **Struts/Shocks (Front Pair)**
- **Book Time:** 2.0-3.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.5-4.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Hard
- **Add:** Alignment required after (+1 hr, $80-150)

**Strut assembly (complete):**
- Parts: $200-500 per pair
- Faster install (no spring compression)

**Strut cartridge only:**
- Parts: $100-250 per pair
- Requires spring compression (add 1-2 hrs)

**Total cost (front pair):**
- Complete assemblies: $500-900
- Cartridge only: $400-800

---

### **Control Arms (Front Lower, Each)**
- **Book Time:** 1.0-2.0 hrs each
- **Real Time:** 1.5-2.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Add:** Alignment required

**Parts:** $80-250 each (with bushings and ball joint)

---

### **Ball Joint Replacement (Each)**
- **Book Time:** 1.5-2.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.0-4.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Variables:** Press-in vs bolt-on

**Note:** Often easier to replace entire control arm than press out ball joint

---

## Transmission

### **Transmission Fluid Change (Automatic)**
- **Book Time:** 0.5-1.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.5-1.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy to Moderate
- **Method:** Drain and fill (partial) vs complete flush

**Fluid quantity:**
- Drain/fill: 4-6 qts
- Complete flush: 10-14 qts

**Fluid cost:**
- Standard ATF: $8-12/qt
- Synthetic/special: $15-30/qt

**Labor:** $60-150

---

### **Clutch Replacement (Manual)**
- **Book Time:** 6.0-10.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 7.0-14.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Add:** Flywheel resurfacing ($80-150) or replacement

**Variables:**
- FWD vs RWD (FWD often requires subframe drop)
- AWD adds complexity

**Common examples:**
- Honda Civic FWD: 6.5 hrs
- BMW E90 RWD: 8.5 hrs
- Subaru WRX AWD: 12 hrs

**Parts:**
- Clutch kit: $200-600
- Flywheel: $150-500 (if replacing)

**Total cost:** $1000-2500

---

## Electrical

### **Battery Replacement**
- **Book Time:** 0.2-0.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 0.2-1.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Add:** Coding/registration (BMW, Audi) +$50-150

**Parts:** $100-250 depending on size/type

---

### **Alternator Replacement**
- **Book Time:** 1.0-3.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 1.5-4.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Hard
- **Variables:** Accessibility

**Easy access (top of engine):** 1.5 hrs
**Poor access (under intake, bottom):** 3-4 hrs

**Parts:**
- Remanufactured: $150-350
- New: $250-500

---

### **Starter Replacement**
- **Book Time:** 1.0-4.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 1.5-6.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Very Hard
- **Variables:** Top accessible vs under/behind engine

**Examples:**
- Honda 4-cyl (top): 1.5 hrs
- BMW V8 (under intake): 6 hrs

**Parts:**
- Remanufactured: $120-250
- New: $200-400

---

## Cooling System

### **Radiator Replacement**
- **Book Time:** 2.0-4.0 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.5-5.0 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Add:** Coolant flush included

**Parts:**
- Aftermarket radiator: $100-250
- OEM: $200-500

---

### **Water Pump Replacement**

**Serpentine belt driven:**
- **Book Time:** 2.0-3.5 hrs
- **Real Time:** 2.5-4.5 hrs
- **Difficulty:** Moderate

**Timing belt driven:**
- **Book Time:** Same as timing belt (4.0-8.0 hrs)
- **Real Time:** Always do with timing belt
- **Difficulty:** Hard

**Note:** If timing belt car needs water pump, always do with timing belt job

**Parts:**
- Aftermarket: $40-100
- OEM: $80-200

## Labor Rate Adjustments

### **By Vehicle Type:**
- **Economy (Honda, Toyota, Nissan):** Base rate
- **Domestic (Ford, GM, Chrysler):** Base rate to +10%
- **European (VW, BMW, Audi, Mercedes):** +20-40%
- **Luxury (Porsche, Land Rover, Jaguar):** +40-60%
- **Exotic (Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren):** +100-200%

### **By Shop Type:**
- **Independent:** $80-130/hr
- **Chain (Firestone, Pep Boys):** $90-140/hr
- **Dealer:** $120-200/hr
- **Specialty (European, exotic):** $150-250/hr

### **By Region:**
- **Rural:** -20%
- **Suburban:** Base
- **Urban:** +20%
- **High cost areas (CA, NY):** +30-50%

## Real-World Time Modifiers

**Add time for:**
- Rusted/seized bolts: +10-50%
- First time doing job: +20-40%
- Broken tools/parts: +varies
- Diagnosis time: Separate charge

**Reduce time for:**
- Experienced tech on familiar car: -10-20%
- Second identical job same day: -15%

---

## Shop Supplies / Misc Fees

**Typical additional charges:**
- Shop supplies: 10-15% of labor
- Waste disposal: $5-15
- Alignment: $80-150 (separate service)
- AC recharge: $100-200
